Counting rules: academia + industry collaborations are their own sector. A model built by several labs counts once for each lab in the lab views. One company's research units count as one lab: Google, Google Brain, Google Research and DeepMind are Google, Facebook AI Research and Meta AI are Meta, and Microsoft Research (including its Asia lab) is Microsoft. A lab's color is the country it is listed under on most of its models. Source: Epoch AI, Notable AI Models.
