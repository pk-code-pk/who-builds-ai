Who builds AI models?

In about ten years, the AI models that matter went from being built mostly by universities to being built by a few US and Chinese companies. So where AI goes next depends on a few firms, and on chip policy between two governments.

    What the glow means

    • The blue bar on the left edge is the US. It runs 57% of the way up the chip, the share of notable AI models from 2024 to 2026 with a US developer and no Chinese one (166 of 289). The circuits on that edge glow blue at the same strength.
    • The red bar on the right edge is China. It runs 32% of the way up, China's share of the same models (92 of 289). Its circuits glow red at that strength.
    • The circuit pulses 22 times when the page opens, once for each US export-control event on AI chips in our list (2018 to 2026). The line under the chip names each one.
    • Pointing at a part lights it in that section's color. The colors match the story: green for universities, amber for companies, blue for the US, red for China, violet for the two together.
