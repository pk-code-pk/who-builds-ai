Who builds AI models?

In about ten years, the AI models that matter went from being built mostly by universities to being built by a few US and Chinese companies. So where AI goes next depends on a few firms, and on chip policy between two governments.

    What the glow means

    • The blue bar on the left side of the chip is the US. It runs 57% of the way from the front edge to the back, the share of notable AI models from 2024 to 2026 with a US developer and no Chinese one (166 of 289).
    • The red bar on the right side is China. It runs 32% of the way back, China's share of the same models (92 of 289). The dark slot under each bar is 100%.
    • The die and the edge lights pulse 22 times when the page opens, once for each US export-control event on AI chips in our list (2018 to 2026). The line under the chip names each one.
    • Pointing at a part lights it in that section's color, and so does pointing at its name in the section menu. The colors match the story: green for universities, amber for companies, blue for the US, red for China, violet for the two together.
